Islamabad City

Islamabad, the meticulously planned capital of Pakistan,
is celebrated for its lush greenery and exceptionally peaceful atmosphere. Nestled against the backdrop of the majestic Margalla Hills, the city seamlessly blends modern infrastructure with stunning natural landscapes. It is systematically divided into organized sectors, housing important political institutions and iconic landmarks such as the Faisal Mosque. As the administrative and diplomatic hub of the country, it offers a high standard of living and a vibrant, diverse culture.
